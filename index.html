Laws of Power

A persistent nation-sim on Discord. Run a country in an invented world, trade with rivals, break treaties, and go to war. The game master never plays a side.

Join the founding players

How the world works

Every player leads a nation on one shared map. The map is never reset. What you build and what you break stays in the world.

No nation is self-sufficient

Each country is short on something it needs. Your economy depends on other players, so diplomacy is how you survive.

War is settled by formula

Army strength, your economy and a measure of chance decide battles. Nobody argues with the GM about who won.

The GM stays neutral

Game masters run the world and the rules. They never lead a nation, so every deal and betrayal comes from players.

One persistent map

No seasonal resets. Alliances, grudges and empires carry forward.

Where it stands

More than 100 players have taken part in Laws of Power before. We are now rebuilding the rules from the ground up, based on what worked and what broke. A small founding team is running a manual test with spreadsheets and Discord, then opening closed testing to more players.
